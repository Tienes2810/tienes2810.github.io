VinSpace (Sep–Oct 2026) · 1 of 3 selected from 1,000 Cohort 3 trainees · onboard vision software for a CubeSat 8U satellite, targeting NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano

  • Solved 96.7% of 2,304 simulated flight-camera frames within 10″ with zero mis-points ≥1° (ESA tetra3: 86.0%; LOST: 76 mis-points) by building a no-ML lost-in-space star tracker with K-vector Pyramid star ID, SVD Wahba, and a validation gate.
  • Sustained 1 Hz attitude solutions on a CPU-only Jetson Orin Nano (3,656/3,656 full-res 24.6 MP frames at ~0.74 s, 1.9 GiB, 6.1 W) by porting the solver to a flight package with ground-built catalogs and a CRC-checked 42-byte packet.
  • Solved 6/6 real night-sky photos and 8/8 FLIR lab frames (LOST misidentified 7/8) by recovering focal scale from star geometry alone.
  • Detected the Moon in 91.4% of 220 held-out real images at 0.9% false alarms (sim-augmented baseline: 83.0% / 4.7%) by curating 437 Moon and 1,162 hard-negative NASA images, splitting by capture session, and retraining YOLOv8n on real plus downscaled-real data.
  • Scoped flight-compatible training data, rejecting 8 of 12 (67%) Earth-image datasets for wrong orbit, sensor, or GSD and selecting BUPT-1 telemetry at 65% input coverage, by scoring every public source against the CubeSat camera and channel list.

Moon Detection & Observation

Finds the Moon in 24.6 MP satellite-camera frames: YOLOv8n (ONNX Runtime) → circle fit on the bright limb for the disc center and off-axis angle → Moon phase from an astronomical ephemeris.
